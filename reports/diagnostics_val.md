   (95% CI: [0.7157, 0.7879]).
2. **View Sensitivity Discrepancy**: Performance differs between radiograph projections:
   Frontal views achieve an ROC-AUC of 0.7655 (95% CI: [0.7237, 0.8053]),

3. **Finding Discrimination**: Across the 10 most frequent abnormal finding tokens, discrimination
   versus normal cases ranges from 0.6342 to 0.9387. All 10 findings have at least
   30 validation images.
4. **Confident Failure Profile**: The most confident false positives reach predicted abnormality
   probabilities up to 0.9479 on normal radiographs. The most confident false negatives
   drop to predicted abnormality probabilities as low as 0.0262 on abnormal radiographs.
